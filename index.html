Who Are We?

Seriously Rugby.
Seriously Netball.
Seriously Social.

Dubai Sharks is a not-for-profit family club — Dubai's home of rugby and netball since 2006. From minis aged 3 to senior men competing in the West Asia Premiership, we're built on grit, grace, and a relentless love of the game. On the pitch we hunt as a pack. Off it, we celebrate like champions. We think everyone looks better in blue.

HOME GROUND

Our Home. The Shark Cage,
Al Habtoor Polo Resort.

Established in 2023, The Shark Cage at Al Habtoor Polo Resort is where Sharks history is made — floodlit pitches, a clubhouse terrace, and the best post-match atmosphere in Dubai. Training runs Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday throughout the season.
